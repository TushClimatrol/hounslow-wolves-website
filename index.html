The club

Built in Hounslow. Hungry every weekend.

The Wolves started in 2020 as a group of mates who wanted proper competitive football with a proper club feel. Six seasons on, we run a Saturday first team in the MCFL Saturday Division One West, two Sunday sides in the NSMBL, and a five-a-side squad that tours the tournament circuit all summer. FA England Accredited, affiliated to Middlesex County FA, and still run by the players.

FA England AccreditedMiddlesex County FACommunity Interest CompanyFA Respect

Competitive football

League and cup football every Saturday and Sunday from September to May, with a physio on the touchline and proper pre-season.

A place for every level

First team standard on Saturdays, two Sunday sides at different levels, and the 5s for anyone who wants fast football and tournament weekends.

One pack, off the pitch too

Socials, end-of-season awards, and a club that turns out for each other. Wembley trips included.

FAQ

Good to know.

What level are the teams?

The Saturday side is our first team and plays in the Middlesex County Football League Division One West. The Sunday 1s are in the NSMBL Premier Division and the Sunday XIs in NSMBL Division One. The 5s play in the Best in West five-a-side league on Monday nights, plus tournament weekends.

How does a trial work?

Request a trial with your previous clubs and level. The manager reviews it and messages you to confirm a date — please don't come down to training until you've had that confirmation. On the day bring boots and shin pads and play. If you're offered a place, your trial fee comes off your first month's subs. We don't run trials for the 5s; that squad is picked from the Saturday and Sunday teams.

What do subs cover?

All training, league and county FA registration, player insurance, referee and pitch fees on match days, and the end-of-season awards night. Kit is a one-off extra at cost.

Can I play for more than one squad?

Yes. Plenty of the Saturday squad also play Sundays, and the 5s draw from all three. One membership covers you across the club.

How much are subs?

Subs are pay as you play and your manager will confirm the current match and training fees when you join. They cover pitch hire, referees, insurance and league registration.

Where do you train and play?

The Saturday and Sunday squads train midweek at Brunel University in Uxbridge; your manager confirms session days and times once you've joined, so please don't turn up unannounced. Saturday home games are on the astro at Hayes & Yeading United FC; Sunday home games are on the grass at Cranford Community College. Maps and directions are in Where we play; pitches are confirmed each week in the squad WhatsApp groups.
